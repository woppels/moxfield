===accordion
===panel: Combo 1: Murderous Redcap + Sac Outlet + Mikaeus

Prerequisites: 
* [[Mikaeus, the Unhallowed|DKA]] on the battlefield.
* [[Umbral Collar Zealot|EOE]] or a sac outlet on the battlefield. 
* [[Murderous Redcap|SHM]] on the battlefield.

Steps: 
1. Activate [[Umbral Collar Zealot|EOE]] and sacrifice [[Murderous Redcap|SHM]] to surveil. Keep the card on top.  
2. [[Murderous Redcap|SHM]]'s Persist trigger and the Undying trigger from [[Mikaeus, the Unhallowed|DKA]] go on the stack. 
3. Choose to resolve the Undying trigger first. 
4. [[Murderous Redcap|SHM]] returns to the battlefield with a +1/+1 counter from Undying and a -1/-1 counter from Persist. These cancel each other out. Use the ETB damage trigger to deal 2 damage to a creature or player of your choice.  
5. Repeat Steps 1 to 4. 

Results: 
* Infinite death triggers
* Infinite ETB
* Infinite LTB
* Infinite sacrifice triggers
* Infinite damage

Variants: 
* If you have [[Flayer of the Hatebound|DKA]] also on the battlefield, you will include additional damage triggers for 3 damage on the Undying trigger resolution and 1 damage on the Persist trigger resolution. 
* Feel free to include other sac outlets in the deck such as [[Warren Soul Trader]] or any of the altar packages such as [[Phyrexian Altar]] or [[Ashnod's Altar]] 
* Include [[Altar of the Brood]] for bonus mill shenanigans. 
===endpanel
===endaccordion

===accordion
===panel: Combo 2: Plague Belcher + Sac Outlet + Mikaeus

Prerequisites: 
* [[Mikaeus, the Unhallowed|DKA]] on the battlefield.
* [[Umbral Collar Zealot|EOE]] or a sac outlet on the battlefield. 
* [[Plague Belcher|AKH]] on the battlefield.

Steps: 
1. Activate [[Umbral Collar Zealot|EOE]] and sacrifice [[Plague Belcher|AKH]] to surveil. Keep the card on top. Each opponent loses 1 life.  
2. The Undying trigger from [[Mikaeus, the Unhallowed|DKA]] goes on the stack. Return [[Plague Belcher|AKH]] to the battlefield.
3. Use the [[Plague Belcher|AKH]] ETB to place two -1/-1 counters on itself. One +1/+1 and -1/-1 counter cancel each other out, leaving a -1/-1 counter. 
4. Repeat Steps 1 to 3. 

Results: 
* Infinite death triggers
* Infinite ETB
* Infinite LTB
* Infinite sacrifice triggers
* Infinite life loss

===endpanel
===endaccordion

===accordion
===panel: Combo 3: Walking Ballista + Mikaeus

Prerequisites: 
* [[Mikaeus, the Unhallowed|DKA]] on the battlefield. 
* [[Walking Ballista]] on the battlefield with at least one +1/+1 counter.  

Steps: 
1. Activate [[Walking Ballista|AER]] and ping any target. 
2. The Undying trigger from [[Mikaeus, the Unhallowed|DKA]] goes on the stack. Return [[Walking Ballista|AER]] to the battlefield with one +1/+1 counter.
3. Repeat Steps 1 and 2. 

Results: 
* Infinite death triggers
* Infinite ETB
* Infinite LTB
* Infinite damage

===endpanel
