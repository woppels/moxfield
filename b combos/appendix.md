===panel: Card Change History

Deck changes are listed in chronological order based on the builds discussed. This records the working decklist decisions, not purchase dates.

### Original 108-core build

- The earlier catalog included [[Doctor Doom, Unrivaled]] + [[Tainted Pact]] as a standalone alternate win condition.
- The earlier decklist also contained [[Bloodline Recollector]] and [[Liliana the Repentant]] before the draw/self-mill experiment.

### Graveyard and card-access package

- **Out:** [[Bloodline Recollector]], [[Liliana the Repentant]]
- **In:** [[Mesmeric Orb]], [[Yawgmoth, Thran Physician]]
- **Reason:** Strengthen self-mill, artifact-to-graveyard triggers with Ultron, sacrifice utility, and repeatable card draw.
- **Combo impact:** Two conditional [[Yawgmoth, Thran Physician]] + [[Out of the Tombs]] configurations were subsequently documented, using [[Zulaport Cutthroat]] or [[Sephiroth, Fabled SOLDIER // Sephiroth, One-Winged Angel]] to offset life payments.

### Artifact-counter combo package (current list)

- **Out:** [[Doctor Doom, Unrivaled]], [[Tainted Pact]]
- **In:** [[Super Shredder]], [[Animation Module]]
- **Reason:** Favor synergistic artifact-creature sacrifice loops and +1/+1 counter production over the Doom/Pact library-empty win condition.
- **Combo impact:** The original Doom/Pact core was retired; five Animation Module combinations appeared in the newer Commander Spellbook export.

### Catalog rebuild and review

- The original 108-core baseline was reconciled with the current decklist.
- The incomplete two-card Nim Deathmantle + Ashnod's Altar entry was retired; separate, complete Deathmantle loops remain.
- Seven further conditional Module/Ooze/Shredder outlet configurations were identified during the later rules review.
- All retained configurations were **renumbered C001–C119**, and the card index was regenerated. The total counts configurations, not necessarily independent minimal win conditions.

### Not part of the confirmed changes

[[Bitter Ordeal]], [[Agatha's Soul Cauldron]], and a potential [[Darksteel Citadel]] substitution were discussed as options, but **none is included in the current submitted decklist**. [[Jet Medallion]] remains in the list.

### Latest supplied decklist

- **Out:** [[Hex Parasite]], [[Overflow Submatrix]]
- **In:** [[Tarrian's Soulcleaver]], [[Voq, Torchbearer]]
- [[Dross Scorpion]] and [[Retrofitter Foundry]] remain.
- This file uses the latest supplied 99 plus [[Ultron the Annihilator]]; no deck changes were made during formatting.
- [[Marionette Apprentice]] is absent; C012 is retained in Archive.
- Historical descriptions above refer to earlier builds, not additional changes to the latest list.

**Interpretation:** The answer under this audit's explicit counting convention is **122 distinct base configurations: 115 unbounded loops and 7 finite sequences**, plus the separately enumerated Voq assembly routes. These are not 122 independent mechanisms or 122 guaranteed wins. The configurations group into **24 mechanical families** (22 with unbounded engines and two consisting of finite sequences). A family is a practical explanation grouping, not a mathematically canonical definition of independence. Alternate outlets/reducers within a family remain separate configurations; optional supersets are merged.

This is a bounded audit of the supplied catalog plus the explicitly described Tarrian/Voq routes. It does not claim exhaustive enumeration of every interaction in the 100-card deck, every generic fuel choice, all opposing graveyards, tutors, Sculpting Steel/Mycosynth Gardens substitutions, or added payoff permutations. The earlier 150–180 projection was not an audited number and should be retired. Do not add Voq routes to 122 and label the result “independent combos.”

## Counting and execution conventions

- **Unbounded:** For any chosen finite number of repetitions, the line restores the resources needed for the next repetition without consuming a finite library, graveyard supply, or life total. Actual play uses finite loop shortcuts. A directly lethal line ends the game before a literal infinite number is performed.
- **Finite:** The stated engine consumes library/graveyard cards or another unreplenished resource. Finite does not mean weak; some finite lines are lethal with enough remaining resources.
- **Lethal:** Enough direct damage or life loss for the remaining opponents under ordinary conditions. Opposing prevention/replacement effects, interaction, hexproof where targets are needed, and effects preventing loss can change this. Mill empties a library; it normally does not eliminate a player until a later failed draw.
- **Required cards:** Every named base/template card is in the supplied list except rejected C012. Extra starter bodies, Zombies, mana artifacts, imprint cards, noncreature-artifact mana value, or empty libraries are explicit requirements even when not included in a legacy entry's title. Consequently a “three-card configuration” is not necessarily a self-contained three-card combo.
- **Deduplication:** Count one exact named card set once; merge a larger engine if the same loop already works after deleting its optional card. Keep alternative mana outlets, reducers, and substantially different resource architectures as distinct configurations. Generic interchangeable fuel bodies do not create more configurations. Some generic bodies can accidentally complete smaller engines; select the specified ordinary fuel if claiming minimality.
- **Setup:** Named permanent engines are on your battlefield unless stated otherwise. You control all fodder and tokens. Initial casting/equip costs have already been paid unless an entry explicitly describes a first cast. No graveyard-exile replacement or effect prevents necessary entries, sacrifices, casting, activation, or life gain. Triggers may be ordered as indicated; opponents receive normal opportunities to respond.
- **Timing:** Repeat creature/artifact casts in your main phase with the stack empty; equip and Mutagen activation are sorcery-speed. Tapping fresh noncreature artifacts for their own abilities is legal. Clock's tap-two-artifacts cost works with newly entered artifact creatures, because it is not those creatures' tap-symbol ability.
- **Outputs:** Repeated token creation does not necessarily accumulate tokens; repeated gain-life events can merely offset a cost. A life-neutral engine does not create an arbitrarily high life total. Choose whether to keep surplus tokens or convert them into mana; do not count the same token as spent twice.

## Material corrections

1. **C012 archived:** Marionette Apprentice is absent. It cannot become available merely by adding Voq unless an opponent's graveyard is assumed; this audit does not assume one.
2. **C063 rejected:** Workshop Assistant and Scrap Trawler have equal mana value, three. Trawler cannot retrieve Assistant. This is an invalid loop, not a mana-seed issue.
3. **Nine Ooze supersets merged:** C054, C056, C069, C071, C074, C088, C095, C102, C103 retain optional utility but are already functioning Plunderer/Stridehangar loops without The Ooze.
4. **Seven finite sequences separated:** C002, C026, C027, C030, C031, C043, C075. Top actually draws a library card each iteration; Ooze/Clock has finite exile feed.
5. **C050/C051 repaired:** Colossus costs eleven; ten total mana value of noncreature artifacts is insufficient for repeated free casts.
6. **Artifact identity matters:** Voq is an artifact on the battlefield when copying an artifact creature, but is a nonartifact Voq card again in the graveyard. Artifact-only return loops cannot blindly recycle him. Deathmantle and Out of the Tombs can return creature cards regardless of artifact type.
7. **The Ooze cannot be copied by Voq:** It is a noncreature artifact, as are Spine, the Altars, Clock, Module, and the Equipment. The earlier suggestion that Voq could copy The Ooze was incorrect.
8. **Soulcleaver triggers once per artifact-creature death:** The wording is one “artifact or creature” trigger. Treasure sacrifices give additional counter events. Its “another” excludes the Equipment itself, not its host.
9. **Ballista must survive paying its ability:** Maintain two initial counters for the Gravecrawler lines and three for Stridehangar's buffed 2/2 Thopter line. At zero toughness Ballista dies before a restoring trigger can save it.
10. **Seed and mana improvements:** C082 needs one floating mana before casting the seven-mana Spine; its Spawn arrives too late to fund that same cast. C059–C062 may sacrifice Spawns to Ashnod for two; KCI cannot sacrifice those nonartifact Spawns. C021's Carrion Feeder already supplies its additional Zombie. C031 deals damage on both Top and Spawn entering.

Phyrexian Altar alone supplies [[symbol:b]]; Pitiless Plunderer supplies a Treasure; The Ghoul supplies a Treasure when its death trigger targets YOU. ### F02 — Top engines — finite library

If the starting library contains L cards, at most L safe iterations occur without an added draw replacement/replenishment effect. These are finite sequences, not unbounded cast/draw engines.

Cloud Key naming Artifact, Foundry Inspector, or Anvil imprinting an artifact makes Top free through Forge. In C031, float [[symbol:1]]; each Top cast creates a Spawn that can sacrifice itself for [[symbol:c]] to fund the next cast. If s spells preceded the loop, N iterations give net N*s + N*(N-1)/2 life. Reservoir shots cost 50 each, and paying to zero life normally loses the game; reserve at least one life. This can be lethal well before exhausting the library, but available library and life determine its damage budget.

### F22 — The Ooze / Animation Module counter transfer

Entries: C113, C114, C119.

With Ashnod/KCI, start with an extra body (artifact creature for KCI), an untapped Ooze, one targetable graveyard card, and two mana. Tap Ooze to exile that card and create Mutagen. During your main phase with an empty stack, spend one to activate Mutagen targeting the starter; spend one on the resulting Module trigger to create a Servo. Sacrifice the countered starter for two; Ooze creates Mutagen. Spend those two to put a counter on the Servo and make a new Servo, then sacrifice the countered Servo and repeat.

Each cycle generates two and spends two: mana-neutral. Each countered body produces one Mutagen; each Module payment produces one Servo, both consumed in the next cycle. C119 uses Phyrexian Altar plus Plunderer instead: the sacrifice produces one Altar mana and a Treasure, together funding the same two expenses. It is also neutral, not surplus mana. Every Mutagen activation waits for an empty stack; seed graveyard fuel is not needed again after the first countered sacrifice. Ultron adds lethal artifact-death drains to the repeatable engine.

### F23 — Soulcleaver / Module Servo replacement

Entries: T001, T002, T003, T004, T005, T006, T007, T008, T009.

T001–T009 each use Soulcleaver already attached to a creature that remains alive. Casting Equipment costs [[symbol:1]] and equipping costs [[symbol:2]]; these are setup costs, not loop expenses. A dead artifact creature triggers Soulcleaver once, not twice. The Equipment itself must remain on the battlefield; its word “another” excludes Soulcleaver itself, not the equipped creature. If its host dies, it cannot put useful counters on that departed host. Each separate Treasure sacrifice can create a further counter event and hence a separate optional Module trigger; this is why trigger ordering and declining unpaid extras matter.

T001/T002 generate two and spend one; T003 generates and spends one. T004–T007 use Plunderer's Treasure to pay Module after a nonmana outlet kills a Servo. T008/T009 use Warren's Treasure and a drain creature to cover its life payment. All restore creature fodder; no finite library or graveyard fuel is consumed by the sustained loop. Each entry below gives exact outlet prerequisites. Equipment variants with Feeder/Ravager plus Plunderer contain existing C106/C109, so they are extensions, not added cores. Adding Ooze, another payoff, or another Equipment to a working smaller loop is likewise not counted again.

### F24 — Soulcleaver / Walking Ballista resource recycling

Entries: T010, T011, T012, T013, T014.

T010/T011 use Ballista as the repeatable Gravecrawler-killing outlet. T013 applies the same two-counter-restored/one-counter-spent idea to a Module-created Servo, paying Module with Plunderer's Treasure. Ballista must retain a counter before any trigger resolves: use at least TWO initial counters. The spare counter each cycle can hit opponents.

T012 kills Stridehangar's 2/2 Thopter; T014 kills Imotekh's 2/2 Necrons. Each kill spends two counters and must start with THREE, leaving one while damage resolves. Creature death and Treasure sacrifice restore two counters; the Treasure's mana funds the resource engine and leaves surplus. Convert mana through Ballista's [[symbol:4]] counter ability to damage opponents. These are five explicit outlet configurations within one explanation family, not five independent mechanisms. Only T013 requires Animation Module. Voq copying Ballista normally enters with zero counters (X=0) and dies before it can be equipped; that is not an accepted substitution.

## Audit limits

This is a manual rules-and-resource audit with programmatic checks of card presence, ID coverage, exact named-set duplication, and count reconciliation. It is not a game-engine simulation, formal proof of all interactions, exhaustive combo search, win-rate measurement, or verification against every possible opposing board. The generic-fuel convention prevents a unique absolute count of mathematically minimal complete card sets. The reported 122 is reproducible for the specified catalog and new entries under the conventions above. Creature cards in opponents' graveyards and unrequested deck changes were excluded.
